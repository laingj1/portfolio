Baby Tracker

  • React
  • PWA
  • Offline-first

Problem: Getting ready for our first baby, my wife and I knew we'd need a shared source of truth. Feeds, sleep, and diaper changes are easy to lose track of at 3 a.m., and "when was the last feeding?" is a hard question to answer from memory. Once daycare entered the picture, the problem grew: we needed to compare what happened at home with what happened during the day to spot real patterns. Also again, we did not want yet another subscription.

Approach: An offline-first PWA with big tap targets, timestamped logs, and a daily view to spot patterns.

What I directed
The whole interaction design for tired hands, offline data strategy, and privacy (everything stays on-device).
What AI handled
Service-worker setup, state management scaffolding, and repetitive UI code.
